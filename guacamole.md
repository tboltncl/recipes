# Guacamole 
## Ingredients (metric)
- avocado (£0.90)
- lime    (£0.45)
- salt    (£0.20)
## Instructions 
1. Cut Avocado and scoop the flesh into a bowl.
2. Add the juice of one lime and the salt.
3. Mash with fork until smooth or leave slightly chunky, according to preference.
4. Taste and adjust lime juice as needed.
5. Serve immediately.
6.  Clean up.
