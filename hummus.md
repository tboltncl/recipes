# Hummus 

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount | Approximate Budget (GBP) |
|------------|---------|--------------------------|
| Chickpeas (canned) | 1 can (400 g / 15 oz), drained and rinsed | £0.60–£1.00 |
| Tahini | 60 ml (1/4 cup) | £0.40–£0.80 |
| Lemon juice | 2 tbsp | £0.10–£0.20 |
| Garlic | 1 clove, minced | £0.03–£0.08 |
| Olive oil | 2 tbsp | £0.10–£0.25 |
| Cold water | 2–4 tbsp | Negligible |
| Ground cumin (optional) | 1/2 tsp | £0.02–£0.05 |
| Salt | 1/2 tsp | Negligible |

**Estimated total cost:** approximately **£1.25–£2.40** for one batch (about 4 servings).

### Ingredient Substitutions

| Original Ingredient | Possible Substitutions |
|---------------------|------------------------|
| Chickpeas | Cannellini beans, butter beans, cooked lentils, edamame |
| Tahini | Sunflower seed butter, cashew butter, almond butter, Greek yogurt |
| Lemon juice | Lime juice, white wine vinegar, apple cider vinegar |
| Garlic | Garlic powder (1/4 tsp), roasted garlic, shallot |
| Olive oil | Avocado oil, rapeseed oil, sunflower oil |
| Ground cumin | Smoked paprika, coriander, za'atar, or omit |
| Salt | Sea salt, kosher salt, low-sodium salt blend |

## Instructions

1. Add the chickpeas, tahini, lemon juice, garlic, olive oil, cumin, and salt to a food processor.
2. Blend until the mixture becomes smooth.
3. Add cold water 1 tablespoon at a time while blending until the desired consistency is reached.
4. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed.
5. Transfer to a serving bowl.
6. Drizzle with additional olive oil and garnish with paprika, chopped parsley, or whole chickpeas if desired.

## Serving Suggestions

- Serve with pita bread.
- Use as a dip for vegetables.
- Spread on sandwiches or wraps.

## Yield

Approximately 4 servings.
